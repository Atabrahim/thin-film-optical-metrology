# Thickness inference and identifiability

The forward model predicts reflectance R (dimensionless) or ellipsometric angles
Psi and Delta (degrees). The inverse problem varies one or two film thicknesses
d (nm), with all refractive indices fixed. It minimizes

\[
Q(d)=\sum_i [(f_i(d)-y_i)/\sigma_i]^2.
\]

Here y is an observation, f is a prediction, and sigma is its declared weighting
scale in the same units. Delta differences use the shortest signed circular
distance in [-180°, 180°). This residual is appropriate for small phase errors
away from vanishing reflected fields; it is not a full circular-noise likelihood.
Reflectance observations affected by noise can fall outside [0,1]; they are retained
and flagged, never silently clipped.

SciPy bounded nonlinear least squares uses central finite differences and a
deterministic Cartesian grid of starting thicknesses. Distinct converged minima
are retained. Multistart reduces starting-point dependence but does not prove a
global optimum. A one-thickness objective scan provides an additional check.
For multiple free layers, holding one fixed is a **conditional slice**, not a
nuisance-parameter profile confidence interval.

## What the uncertainty does and does not mean

For a locally linear, full-rank model, covariance is `(J.T @ J)^-1`, where J is the
Jacobian of weighted residuals with respect to thickness in nm. An SVD avoids
forming and inverting the squared-condition normal matrix.

- `absolute_sigma=True`: sigma is assumed known independent Gaussian measurement
  standard deviation. Covariance is conditional on that assumption.
- `absolute_sigma=False`: sigma supplies relative weights; covariance is multiplied
  by `Q / (number of observations - number of parameters)`. This is an estimated
  iid residual-noise model, not instrument calibration uncertainty.
- Standard errors are local one-sigma values, not optimizer tolerances or proof of
  a unique thickness. Other minima can be far outside those intervals.
- Active bounds suppress unconstrained covariance. An SVD ratio below 1e-7 also
  suppresses it: this conservative numerical-rank threshold avoids manufacturing
  information from finite-difference noise. Condition number and parameter
  correlation remain important even above this threshold.
- Competing minima are flagged using a chi-square 95% increment with p fitted
  parameters for known sigma, or a 5% objective heuristic otherwise. This is a
  screening diagnostic, not a guaranteed multimodal confidence region.
- Lag-one residual correlation above 0.3 is flagged as a heuristic warning. It is
  not a formal independence test; adjacent spectral errors can be correlated.

Material dispersion, angle calibration, surface roughness and model discrepancy
are absent from this local covariance. Sensitivity refits should be reported
separately. In particular, the measured TiO2 dispersion was fitted by the source
to the same spectra: the reanalysis is conditional, not an independent thickness
measurement. See [data provenance](../data/PROVENANCE.md).

## Independent checks

Single-film observations are generated by the analytical Airy intensity formula,
not this package's forward function. Two-film synthetic ellipsometry is generated
by the independent `tmm` implementation. Known-thickness recovery, seeded noise,
wrapped phases, covariance against an independent finite-difference Fisher
calculation, active bounds and deliberately indistinguishable adjacent films
exercise both successful and failed inference. Single-colour data intentionally
produce multiple interference-order solutions.
